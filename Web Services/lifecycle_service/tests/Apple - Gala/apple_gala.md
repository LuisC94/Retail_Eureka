Type 1 (Control / Untreated): No 1-MCP application before or after harvest

Stage 2 - Refrigerated Long-Distance Transport:
- $1^\circ\text{C}$ 
- ~95% RH 
- Normal Atmosphere: $21\%\text{ O}_2, 78\%\text{ N}_2, 0.05\%\text{ CO}_2$ 
- 4 or 6 weeks (28 to 42 days) 
- Minor impact on overall quality loss ($\eta^2 = 1.79\%$ for firmness); acts as a thermal stabilizing buffer. 
- Estimated Remaining Shelf Life between 7 to 14 days

Stage 3 - Simulated Trading & Retail Shelf Life:
- $25^\circ\text{C}$ 
- ~55% RH (Ambient) 
- Normal Atmosphere (Retail market conditions) 
- 0, 7, to 14 days 
- Major driver of quality deterioration ($\eta^2 = 55.5\%$ for firmness loss, $30.1\%$ for TA loss); severe thermal stress.
- Estimated Remaining Shelf Life between 0 to 7 days
