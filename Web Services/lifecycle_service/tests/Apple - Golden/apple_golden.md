Ambient / Shelf-Life Room:
- $1\text{--}2^\circ\text{C}$ -> Temperature
- $95\%$ -> RH
- ~150 days (approx. 5 months) on average of shelf life.
- Medium rate of weight loss ($3\%$ after 100 days). The acceptable flesh firmness threshold of $50\text{ N}$ was reached at ~150 days. Vitamin C and total acids decreased rapidly ($~50\%$ Vitamin C remaining at 5 months).
