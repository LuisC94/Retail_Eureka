🧠 Warehouse State (Observation)

  • RSL Profile (G0-G3): Stock grouped by remaining shelf-life days
  • Expected Demand: Forecast for today (T) and tomorrow (T+1)
  • Base Price: Today's benchmark market price
  • Relative Price: Market price Z-score (last 15 days)
  • Cyclic Signals: Sine/cosine position of day/month
  • Critical Metrics: Stock coverage rate and urgency ratio

💰 Return Equation (Reward)

Reward = Profit / 100 - Penalties
  • Revenue: Daily sales based on dynamic price and elasticity
  • Storage Costs: Daily expense per occupied volume (0.70 €/m³)
  • Spoilage Costs: Acquisition cost (60% of base price) per kilo of biological discard
  • Price Regularization: Penalizes extreme deviations from benchmark market price
  • Exposure Regularization: Penalizes the agent if stock is kept in storage without shelf exposure
